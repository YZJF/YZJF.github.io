Research

LUCiD: Mitigating Hallucinations in Large Language Models with Learned Unilayer Contrastive Decoding

Equal contribution · manuscript under double-blind review · 2026

Freeze the LLM backbone, RMSNorm, and LM head, then train a single middle-layer residual probe and a per-token gate under an adaptive plausibility constraint. Evaluation covers five models from 0.6B to 12B and ten held-out benchmarks.
